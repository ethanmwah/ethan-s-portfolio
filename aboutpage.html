about

ethan thompson is an artist from massachusetts with a focus on 16mm and digital filmmaking, as well as stills and photography from film strips and said mediums.

his work often contains themes of dreams, movement, and personal experiences that are either muted or dramatized. he works best under pressure and usually ideas are founded day of and fleshed out throughout the creative process. his short film "baltz/waltz/oneiric" recently premiered at the 2025 five college film festival were it was in competition for best experimental film.

he is currently working on a somewhat religious piece that will focus on his individual relationship with God, and the world's relationship with Him.
